Showcasing Gaussian Process's Unique Capability: Prediction Uncertainty
====================================================================================================

WHY THIS MATTERS:
--------------------------------------------------
Traditional classifiers (LR, RFC) output only point predictions.
GP provides UNCERTAINTY estimates, enabling:
  1. Risk stratification (Low/Medium/High confidence)
  2. Flagging uncertain cases for human review
  3. Confidence-filtered predictions with higher accuracy
  4. Better clinical decision support
